# Prompt Set

## Character portraits

The 20 character images use the `stylized-concept` use case and the same
polished, realistic science-fiction game-concept finish as the exterior art.
Each is a landscape 16:9, waist-up three-quarter portrait of one representative
individual, not a named ruler. The matching faction ship concept was supplied
as the visual reference. Shared constraints exclude readable text, watermarks,
trademarks, flags, heraldry, real celebrities, and recognizable existing
science-fiction franchises.

- **01 — Veridian Combine:** male and female survey-claims officers in graphite
  pressure suits with pale-alloy hexagonal modules, muted emerald instruments,
  and calm corporate authority inside a survey registry bay.
- **02 — Ferrous Hegemony:** male and female frontier officers in symmetrical
  iron-gray armor with restrained crimson bands and brass service accents,
  framed by a formal cruiser readiness deck.
- **03 — Freehold Compact:** male and female homesteader lane-keepers in
  repaired cream, faded-blue, and barn-red work gear inside a warm community
  workshop and greenhouse.
- **04 — Red Ledger:** male and female tribute captains/bookkeepers in rebuilt
  dark armor, dried-red tally geometry, and tarnished copper, working from an
  amber-lit raider counting room.
- **05 — Gilded Chain:** male- and female-presenting reptilian auction-house
  representatives in immaculate black-ceramic, ivory, and old-gold formalwear,
  lit by cold turquoise transfer galleries.
- **06 — The Beautiful Ones:** male- and female-presenting nonhuman Vow-keepers:
  extremely tall, slender alien humanoids with elongated necks, near-knee arm
  reach, long digitigrade legs, four-digit hands, hairless sensory crowns, dark
  almond eyes, reduced breathing slits, opalescent skin, and gentle
  bioluminescence. Their pearl-and-indigo grown pressure harnesses and living
  sanctuary share the companion ships' visual language; graceful and tender,
  never body horror.
- **07 — The Unknowables:** male- and female-presenting temporary humanoid field
  approximations made only from ultraviolet/cyan plasma, white-gold nodes,
  lensing arcs, charged dust, anchor matter, and energy cells. The female final
  uses wholly abstract, non-anatomical field geometry.
- **08 — The Assembly:** male- and female-presenting diplomatic survey avatars,
  clearly mechanical contact chassis of weathered off-white alloy, charcoal,
  faded orange copy-error panels, teal optics, recursive modules, and antennae.
- **09 — Congregation of the Further Shore:** male and female pilgrim navigators
  in practical midnight-blue and weathered-silver sacred pressure garments,
  with candle-amber lamps and violet Wakeglass navigation optics.
- **10 — Lamplighter Guild:** male and female gatewright technicians in
  soot-dark work suits with replaceable utility-yellow panels, amber lamps,
  cobalt diagnostics, tools, cable reels, and gate-service gantries.

## Shared direction

All 30 images used the `stylized-concept` use case and were requested as polished, realistic science-fiction concept art suitable for game visual development. They use a wide three-quarter exterior composition, keep the complete primary silhouette readable, and avoid readable text, watermarks, trademarks, finalized flags/heraldry, and resemblance to existing science-fiction franchises.

Each faction received three separate prompts: a station, a representative ship, and a jump gate or faction-contextualized gate encounter. The following is the condensed final prompt set; each bullet lists the visual language followed by the three asset subjects.

## 01 — Veridian Combine

Corporate extraction, surveys, patents, and calm legal authority. Graphite and pale alloy, muted green light, precise sensors, modular hexagonal industrial construction.

- Station: immense extraction-and-survey complex with assay towers, ore silos, docking spokes, and registry infrastructure.
- Ship: long-range survey and claim-enforcement vessel with armored sample bays, detachable survey pods, and precision sensor fins.
- Jump gate: segmented hexagonal gate with inspection docks, assay modules, claim beacons, and controlled emerald-white aperture.

## 02 — Ferrous Hegemony

Militarist frontier state expressed through monumental armored geometry, disciplined symmetry, iron gray, restrained crimson, and brass recognition accents.

- Station: fortress-bastion and readiness depot with radial batteries, formal inspection ground, and central command tower.
- Ship: line cruiser with blunt reinforced prow, layered citadel plating, formal weapon housings, and rescue-capable hangar.
- Jump gate: armored checkpoint ring with four cardinal bastions, blast shutters, inspection docks, and exact fleet formations.

## 03 — Freehold Compact

Homesteader civic cooperation: repaired modules, mismatched but cared-for parts, warm windows, barn red, weathered cream, faded blue, and bare metal.

- Station: community habitat grown from farm rings, workshops, water tanks, greenhouse modules, and donated hull sections.
- Ship: multipurpose homestead freighter for hauling, rescue, family travel, and local defense.
- Jump gate: community lifeline surrounded by replaced ring segments, repair scaffolds, greenhouse service pods, and warm beacons.

## 04 — Red Ledger

Organized piracy with an accountant's soul: rebuilt captured hardware, deliberate tally-like geometry, dark iron, dried-blood red, tarnished copper, and amber utility light.

- Station: fortified freeport, ransom exchange, tribute vault, and counting house built around a captured refinery core.
- Ship: raider-command vessel with boarding prow, grappling booms, cargo clamps, hidden gun ports, and deliberate repair scars.
- Jump gate: tribute choke point assembled from captured gate segments with armored toll platforms, boarding docks, and strict beacon lanes.

## 05 — Gilded Chain

Reptilian auction-house elegance concealing institutional horror: scale-like armor, black ceramic, ivory alloy, old gold, and cold turquoise illumination.

- Station: immaculate orbital auction pavilion with sealed catalog vaults, transfer bays, docking galleries, and observation rotunda.
- Ship: refined acquisition vessel with overlapping armor, sealed transfer galleries, precision tractor arrays, and hidden defenses.
- Jump gate: exclusive customs threshold with oval scale-segment ring, symmetrical inspection salons, and pale turquoise aperture.

## 06 — The Beautiful Ones

Living companion ships as kin and religious participants: grown bone, muscular membrane, healed tissue, symbiotic gardens, pearl and indigo surfaces, cyan-violet bioluminescence; majestic and tender, never body horror.

- Station: living sanctuary shaped as protective petals around a warm central lagoon, with cradle-like docks and clutch chambers.
- Ship: intelligent manta-whale-like adult companion with breathing vents, expressive sensory crown, healed scars, and luminous veins.
- Jump gate: old machinery peacefully enclosed by living bone-and-membrane petals and gentle cradle branches.

## 07 — The Unknowables

Human approximations of nonhuman energy configurations: stable fields, lensing, charged dust, sparse anchor matter, ultraviolet, electric cyan, and white-gold light.

- Station: persistent exchange locus of levitating energy-cell vaults, dark anchors, toroidal fields, plasma sheets, and synchronized pulses.
- Ship: coherent traveling field carrying physical energy cells inside nested magnetic loops with no hull or cockpit.
- Jump gate: human gate transformed during transit by gravitational lens arcs, plasma geometries, and floating energy cells.

## 08 — The Assembly

Ancient self-replicating survey-probe descendants with generational copy errors: recursive modules, weathered off-white alloy, charcoal, faded orange, and teal optics.

- Station: archive-and-replication habitat of repeating foundry cells, data vaults, antenna forests, and an ancient central probe body.
- Ship: uncrewed deep-survey vessel with sensor crown, observation petals, fabrication bays, and self-similar daughter probes.
- Jump gate: industrial ring surrounded by recursive scaffolds, duplicated archives, self-building gantries, and probe constellations.

## 09 — Congregation of the Further Shore

Outward-facing pilgrim faith: practical sacred architecture, radial rimward orientation, midnight blue, weathered silver, candle amber, and violet Wakeglass reflections.

- Station: frontier refuge and observatory with outward-facing nave, sect-specific modular bays, pilgrim docks, and deep-rim sensors.
- Ship: long-range pilgrimage vessel with forward observation chamber, archive vault, redundant life support, and folded rescue sails.
- Jump gate: sacred horizon surrounded by practical shrines, refuge docks, outward sensors, and disciplined amber guidance lights.

## 10 — Lamplighter Guild

Neutral infrastructure priesthood and labor guild: standardized replaceable parts, exposed access routes, soot-black metal, weathered utility yellow, warm lamps, and cobalt diagnostics.

- Station: gate-service depot with spare ring segments, relay masts, cranes, parts yards, and repair docks.
- Ship: rugged gate tender with articulated repair arms, cable reels, modular workshops, tug engines, and relay mast.
- Jump gate: indispensable frontier ring with service gantries, relay crown, maintenance rail, tugs, shutters, and thousands of amber guide lamps.
