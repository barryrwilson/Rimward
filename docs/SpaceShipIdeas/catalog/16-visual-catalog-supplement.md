# Visual catalog — lead pass (images viewed directly)

Scope: sixteen ships that the lead looked at one by one, at full size, outside the contact sheets.
This pass covers the strongest role references and replaces the catalog rows where an automatic
lookup returned a faction logo instead of a hull.
Accessed: 2026-08-12

---

### S-01 — Millennium Falcon (Star Wars)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starwars/images/5/52/Millennium_Falcon_Fathead_TROS.png/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Disc with a forward mandible fork and an offset cockpit tube on the right side. The shape is unmistakable in one glance because it breaks radial symmetry once, in one place.
- **Proportions:** About 1.0 length : 0.9 width : 0.22 height. The mass sits mid and low; the fork is empty volume.
- **Volume breakdown:** Disc body ~70 %, mandibles ~12 %, cockpit tube ~6 %, top and bottom turret blisters ~6 %, engine deck ~6 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Three big forms, about ten medium forms (bay covers, sensor dish, quad turrets, escape hatch rings), then a dense field of small greeble. Greeble concentrates in trenches between hull plates and around the engine deck. The top and bottom plate centres stay smooth.
- **Surface + colour:** Two-tone light grey with pale sand highlights. Panels are large: a single plate can span one sixth of the hull width. Red-brown accent blocks appear on maybe 4 % of the surface and read as repair patches, not as livery.
- **Light language:** Very little emissive area. One cockpit window band, a few navigation points. The ship reads by shape and value, not by glow.
- **Function reads:** The dish says sensors, the fork says cargo grapple, the quad turrets say armed civilian, and the flat plate top says the hull is a pressure lid over machinery.
- **Scale cues:** Boarding ramp, hatch rings, and handrails around the dish.
- **Transferable move:** Break symmetry exactly once on a `light` or `cutter` hull. Put the cockpit off-axis and leave the rest of the hull symmetrical. Freehold Compact benefits most.

### S-02 — MCRN Donnager (The Expanse)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/expanse/images/1/1b/Donnager_Render_1.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Long spine with a stepped, wedge-shaped bow and four large flat fins near the stern. It reads as a tower laid on its side, because it is a thrust-axis ship.
- **Proportions:** About 5.5 : 1.4 : 1. Mass sits aft of centre. The drive block is the widest part.
- **Volume breakdown:** Central spine ~45 %, drive and tank block ~25 %, four fin panels ~15 %, bow command wedge ~10 %, hangar boxes ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Five big forms, about 20 medium boxes, then very dense fine greeble on every flank. About 70 % of the surface carries fine detail. Only the fin faces stay flat.
- **Surface + colour:** Near-black grey with rust-orange accents at edges and inside recesses. Plate size is small: each plate spans about one fortieth of the hull length, which makes the ship read as huge.
- **Light language:** Almost no window grid. A thin warm line marks the open hangar mouth. Emissive share below 2 %.
- **Function reads:** Radiator fins, torpedo hatches, a rail-gun spine, and a hangar deck mouth. The drive end is a machine, not a nozzle sculpture.
- **Scale cues:** Hangar mouth with deck lighting, small hull hatches, ladder rails.
- **Transferable move:** For `frigate`, build the hull as a thrust tower: bow crew wedge, mid weapons spine, aft drive block, and flat radiator fins that break the outline. Ferrous Hegemony and Red Ledger both fit.

### S-03 — Battlestar Galactica, Jupiter class (2003 series)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/galactica/images/7/76/Jupiter-class_Transparent.png/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Alligator head plus a ribbed spine and two flight pods carried outboard on pylons.
- **Proportions:** About 4 : 1.6 : 0.9. The mass sits mid; the pods push the width.
- **Volume breakdown:** Central hull ~55 %, two flight pods ~30 %, head ~10 %, pylons ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Three big forms, then a strong medium rhythm: about 40 armour ribs run across the flanks at even spacing. Small greeble is sparse. The rib rhythm carries the whole design.
- **Surface + colour:** Warm grey armour with darker wash in the rib valleys. Faded red bands and a squadron number sit at 3 % of the area. Weathering is heavy and directional: streaks run aft.
- **Light language:** Landing bay interiors glow warm red. Outside light is minimal.
- **Function reads:** Flight pods say carrier, ribs say armour belts, the head says command and sensors.
- **Scale cues:** Bay mouths with visible deck rows, small window slits at the head.
- **Transferable move:** Use one repeated structural rib as the main mid-form for a `heavy` hull. Twenty to forty ribs at even pitch produce mass without extra greeble. Good for Ferrous Hegemony.

### S-04 — Type-9 Heavy (Elite Dangerous)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/elite-dangerous/images/b/b3/Type-9_Heavy.png/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Flat, broad slab with a chamfered bow and a small pod cockpit hung under the nose.
- **Proportions:** About 1.6 : 1.5 : 0.45. The hull is nearly as wide as it is long, which is a freighter signal.
- **Volume breakdown:** Cargo deck slab ~65 %, upper superstructure ~15 %, side sponsons ~12 %, cockpit pod ~4 %, drives ~4 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Two big forms, about a dozen medium hatch panels, and modest fine greeble. Large smooth areas dominate, which makes the painted stripe do the work.
- **Surface + colour:** Cool grey-blue with one orange stripe that runs along the deck edge and turns at right angles. The stripe covers about 5 % of the area and gives the ship its identity.
- **Light language:** Warm amber strip lights along the lower flanks, plus deck marker lights. Emissive share about 4 %.
- **Function reads:** Big flat deck, hatch covers, low-slung cockpit, and station handling lights. The ship says "carries containers", not "fights".
- **Scale cues:** Landing gear bays, hangar deck markings, station rails in the same frame.
- **Transferable move:** For `freighter`, keep the width close to the length and hold 60 % of the hull as one flat deck. Paint one continuous turning stripe. Veridian Combine and Lamplighter Guild both suit this.

### S-05 — USCSS Nostromo (Alien)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/avp/images/c/c5/Img4.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Wide industrial tug at the front, with an open truss and a stack of separated engine blocks behind.
- **Proportions:** About 2.2 : 1.6 : 0.8, plus a detachable refinery train that is much larger in the story.
- **Volume breakdown:** Forward tug body ~45 %, four aft engine and tank blocks ~35 %, connecting structure ~15 %, antenna clutter ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Four big forms and heavy medium detail everywhere. Greeble is pipe and duct work rather than panel lines.
- **Surface + colour:** Cold white-grey with black shadow gaps. There is no accent colour at all. Value contrast, not hue, separates parts.
- **Light language:** Two small red points and a few white spots. The ship is almost dark.
- **Function reads:** The vehicle reads as a tug: no weapons, big drives, and a mass of process plumbing.
- **Scale cues:** Small hatch panels and repeated deck rails on the flanks.
- **Transferable move:** For an industrial `heavy` or `freighter`, leave gaps between structural blocks. Empty space between modules reads as size and as function. Freehold Compact and Red Ledger fit.

### S-06 — Serenity, Firefly class
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/firefly/images/1/11/Firefly_class_ship.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Insect body: a round nose bulb, a long neck, a wide belly, two swivel engine pods on stalks, and a glowing tail ring.
- **Proportions:** About 2.6 : 1.5 : 0.9. Mass sits aft of centre at the belly.
- **Volume breakdown:** Belly hull ~40 %, forward bulb and neck ~20 %, two engine nacelles ~20 %, tail drive ring ~12 %, gear and greeble ~8 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Five big forms, then a light dusting of medium detail. Very little fine greeble. The design wins with silhouette.
- **Surface + colour:** Sand-grey with green and brown streaks. Dirt collects behind protrusions.
- **Light language:** One large warm amber ring at the tail, a few green and red navigation lights, and lit cockpit glass. The ring is the whole light identity, at about 6 % of the visible area.
- **Function reads:** Cargo doors, swivel nacelles for atmospheric flight, no guns.
- **Scale cues:** Crew door, ladder, and window rows on the neck.
- **Transferable move:** Give one class a single large emissive ring and no other bright element. A `light` or `cutter` hull becomes instantly recognisable in traffic. Freehold Compact suits it.

### S-07 — Moya, a Leviathan (Farscape)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/farscape/images/0/04/Moya1.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Smooth teardrop shell with two long horn spurs that sweep forward and down.
- **Proportions:** About 2.4 : 1.1 : 0.8. Mass sits forward of centre in the head bulge.
- **Volume breakdown:** Main shell ~70 %, twin horns ~18 %, dorsal ridge ~7 %, small tail forms ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** One big form and a continuous flow of medium ribs. There are no plates and no panel lines. Surface detail follows the body curvature like tendon lines.
- **Surface + colour:** Amber bone and brown, with lighter tendon streaks. Colour changes slowly across the body, which reads as skin, not paint.
- **Light language:** Rows of small white points follow the tendon lines, like portholes grown into the shell.
- **Function reads:** No visible drives, weapons, or radiators. The absence is the message: this is a creature.
- **Scale cues:** Only the light rows, which act as windows.
- **Transferable move:** For Beautiful Ones, run detail along flow lines and forbid straight panel cuts. Place window points on the flow lines, not on a grid.

### S-08 — Amarr Apocalypse (EVE Online)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/eve/images/6/69/Apocalypse512.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Long tapered blade with a raised aft castle. It reads like a cathedral nave laid horizontally.
- **Proportions:** About 4.2 : 1.1 : 1.0. Mass sits aft.
- **Volume breakdown:** Tapered forward body ~55 %, aft castle ~25 %, ventral pod ~12 %, spines and fittings ~8 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Two big forms, about fifteen medium bands, and a repeated small stud detail along every edge. The stud rows read as gold rivets and appear on maybe 15 % of the edges.
- **Surface + colour:** Sand and bone with polished gold trim on the nose cap and the spine. Gold covers about 8 % of the area, always on edges and always in the same shape family.
- **Light language:** Nearly no emissive. Value and metal reflection carry the design.
- **Function reads:** No visible cargo. Everything reads as authority: prow, castle, and ornament.
- **Scale cues:** Stud pitch and repeated arch modules.
- **Transferable move:** For Gilded Chain, place precious-metal trim only along edges and only in one repeated arch motif. Keep the field colour matte and pale.

### S-09 — Minmatar Rifter (EVE Online)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/eve/images/1/1e/Rifter.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Two long parallel hulls held together by exposed cross-braces, with a thin mast forward.
- **Proportions:** About 3.2 : 1.5 : 0.6. Mass sits at the two hulls; the structure between them is open.
- **Volume breakdown:** Twin hull booms ~65 %, cross-frames ~15 %, forward mast ~10 %, greeble and pipes ~10 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Two big forms, then repeated ladder-frame medium detail, then rust and patch noise. The frame rhythm is the design.
- **Surface + colour:** Rust brown over grey, with visible weld seams and mismatched plates.
- **Light language:** Small orange running lights only.
- **Function reads:** Everything is exposed: fuel lines, frames, and thruster nozzles. The ship reads as built from salvage.
- **Scale cues:** Frame pitch and hand-sized fittings on the booms.
- **Transferable move:** For Freehold Compact and Red Ledger, expose the structure. Model open frames between two solid volumes instead of a single closed shell.

### S-10 — Gallente Megathron (EVE Online)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/eve/images/2/2f/Megathron512.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Forked, organic-industrial body with two heavy prongs forward and a raised crest aft.
- **Proportions:** About 2.8 : 1.6 : 1.1. Mass sits mid and low.
- **Volume breakdown:** Twin prongs ~40 %, core body ~35 %, dorsal crest ~15 %, fins ~10 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Three big forms and smooth transitions between them. Medium detail is inset, not added on. Fillets replace hard chamfers.
- **Surface + colour:** Olive and dark bronze. The metal is soft and dark, and the transition between forms is a curve, not a corner.
- **Light language:** Cyan-green slot lights inside recesses. The glow sits in shadow pockets, at about 3 % of the area.
- **Function reads:** The prongs read as drive housings and weapon mounts. The crest reads as sensors.
- **Scale cues:** Recess slots and the inset light rows.
- **Transferable move:** Put emissive strips inside recesses, never on flat outer plates. This is the cheapest way to make a hull look machined rather than painted. Veridian Combine fits.

### S-11 — Pride of Hiigara (Homeworld 2)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/homeworld/images/b/b7/Pride_of_Hiigara.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Tall, blade-like tower with a narrow keel and a spine that grows a service city along one side.
- **Proportions:** About 1 : 0.35 : 3.5 in the shown orientation. The ship is a vertical wall.
- **Volume breakdown:** Main slab ~60 %, service spine with modules ~25 %, forward prow ~10 %, masts ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** One dominant slab, one long medium band of modules, and dense small detail restricted to that band. Roughly 75 % of the hull is smooth, 25 % is busy. The contrast is the reason the ship reads at any distance.
- **Surface + colour:** Sand and teal-grey plates, with dark inset trenches. Warm interior light spills from the module band.
- **Light language:** Amber window rows and small green and cyan markers, all inside the band.
- **Function reads:** The band holds hangars, control rooms, and berths. The clean slab holds structure and armour.
- **Scale cues:** Window rows, docked small craft, and railed catwalks in the band.
- **Transferable move:** Confine 80 % of the greeble and all windows to one narrow band. Use this for every `freighter` and `frigate`. It creates class-level readability across all ten factions.

### S-12 — USS Sulaco (Aliens)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/avp/images/8/8d/Sulaco_approach.JPG/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** A single long girder with weapons and pods hung from it. The hull is a rack, not a tube.
- **Proportions:** About 7 : 1 : 1.2. Mass sits forward at the head block.
- **Volume breakdown:** Spine rack ~50 %, forward command block ~20 %, weapon and hangar pods ~20 %, engine cluster ~10 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Two big forms and about a dozen hung medium pods. Detail lives at the joints where pods meet the spine.
- **Surface + colour:** Cold grey-blue, almost monochrome, with black shadow. No livery is visible.
- **Light language:** Almost none. The ship is a shadow with edge highlights.
- **Function reads:** Hung pods and rails say "military carrier". Nothing says comfort.
- **Scale cues:** Repeated pod size and the drop-ship bay.
- **Transferable move:** Hang modules from a visible external rail. Then a `frigate` can change loadout between missions with no new hull. Ferrous Hegemony and Assembly fit.

### S-13 — Imperial Cutter (Elite Dangerous)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/elite-dangerous/images/8/8e/Cutter_view.png/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Smooth dart with a swept tail and two small outboard pods. The whole shape is one continuous curve.
- **Proportions:** About 3.4 : 1.5 : 0.6. Mass sits forward of centre.
- **Volume breakdown:** Main body ~75 %, tail boom ~12 %, outboard pods ~8 %, fins ~5 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** One big form, few medium forms, and almost no greeble. Detail appears only as dark inset cut-outs, which read as vents and sensor bays.
- **Surface + colour:** Pearl white with a soft metallic sheen. Dark grey inserts cover about 10 % of the area.
- **Light language:** Thin cyan-white lines follow the body curve at the hull break. Emissive share about 2 %, but the lines are long, so they read strongly.
- **Function reads:** Wealth and speed. The lack of visible machinery is the statement.
- **Scale cues:** Only the small cockpit glass and door seams. The ship deliberately hides its size.
- **Transferable move:** For Gilded Chain, hide all machinery under one continuous shell and use long thin light lines that follow the curve. Contrast this with the exposed frames of Freehold Compact.

### S-14 — UNSC Infinity (Halo)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/halo/images/5/59/H4-UNSCInfinity-Starship-Side.png/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Long rectangular block with a stepped upper deck and a flat underside. The side view is almost a bar.
- **Proportions:** About 6.5 : 1.1 : 1. Mass is even along the length, which is a capital-ship read.
- **Volume breakdown:** Main hull box ~60 %, upper deck stack ~20 %, ventral fairings ~12 %, drive block ~8 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Two big forms, about 30 medium boxes along the deck, and continuous fine panel noise. Detail density stays constant along the hull, so length carries the scale.
- **Surface + colour:** Warm grey and dust, with black recesses. Painted ship name in large letters spans about 20 % of the length and gives instant scale.
- **Light language:** One blue drive glow at the stern, small white deck lights elsewhere.
- **Function reads:** Hangar mouths, gun decks, and antenna masts. Every feature repeats along the hull.
- **Scale cues:** Hull lettering, antenna masts, and repeated deck modules.
- **Transferable move:** Paint the ship name or registry at a fixed letter height on every faction hull. Constant letter height across classes turns text into a scale ruler.

### S-15 — Anvil Carrack (Star Citizen)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/starcitizen/images/b/b6/Carrack_-_concept_art_%2812%29.jpg/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Wedge body with a glass command nose, a flat spine, and a deployed rear ramp with masts.
- **Proportions:** About 3 : 1.4 : 0.8. Mass sits mid.
- **Volume breakdown:** Body wedge ~60 %, nose glass block ~12 %, aft ramp and bay ~15 %, masts and pods ~13 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** Three big forms and a small number of medium forms. Detail collects at the bay mouth, where a rover drives out.
- **Surface + colour:** Matte black-grey with a red stripe and hull code near the nose. Snow and dust collect on horizontal faces.
- **Light language:** Cool white interior light from the cockpit glass and the ramp bay. Small red mast lights.
- **Function reads:** Ramp, rover, and masts say exploration and survey.
- **Scale cues:** The rover, the crew figures, and the ramp steps. This is the clearest scale image in the whole set.
- **Transferable move:** Model one open bay with a vehicle or a container inside it. One nested object sells hull size better than any amount of surface detail.

### S-16 — Khar-Kushan (Homeworld 3)
- **Image:** https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/homeworld/images/2/29/Khar-Kushan.JPG/revision/latest
- **Silhouette:** Extremely long, thin lens. Height is a small fraction of length.
- **Proportions:** About 12 : 2 : 1. The ship reads as a horizon line.
- **Volume breakdown:** Main lens hull ~80 %, ventral module strip ~12 %, dorsal fittings ~8 %.
- **Detail hierarchy:** One huge form and one thin strip of dense modules on the underside. Everything else is bare.
- **Surface + colour:** Dark bronze-grey with warm rim light along the top edge. The light does the modelling.
- **Light language:** Small white and warm points along the module strip, spaced at a constant pitch. The pitch itself tells the viewer how long the ship is.
- **Function reads:** The module strip holds docking and service. The bare hull reads as structure.
- **Scale cues:** Constant light pitch along the strip.
- **Transferable move:** Fix a constant light pitch, for example one light per 8 m, and hold it across every class. Then the light count alone communicates ship length.

## Lead-pass conclusions

1. Ships that read at distance share one property: one dominant primary form holds 55–80 % of the volume, and the busy detail is confined to a band or a joint zone.
2. Emissive area is small on almost every strong design. Between 1 % and 6 % of visible area is lit. Shape and value carry the read; light only marks the identity.
3. Scale comes from repeated human-size modules, from nested vehicles, and from constant light or letter pitch. It never comes from more greeble.
4. Faction identity survives at thumbnail size only when it is carried by shape logic (open frame, closed shell, ribbed armour, module band) and one small colour habit, not by texture.
5. Industrial ships expose gaps and structure. Wealthy ships close the shell. Military ships repeat one module. Grown ships remove panel lines. These four rules cover most of the corpus.
