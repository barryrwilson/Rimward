## Outcome and scope

A trader can enter a quantity, fill available space with one commodity, or sell all held units of one commodity without repeating five-unit trades. A rested bulk market and a sufficiently funded 160-unit freighter must support a 160-unit purchase and sale using two ordinary orders each: 99 then 61. The player sees the quantity, executable unit quote, and total before committing, then an exact receipt. Existing +1/+5/−1/−5 buttons and Q/W/A/S remain available.

This is a market interaction change. Preserve #53's corrected same-dock spread and #55's approved liquidity contract in [MarketLiquidityProgressionDesign.md](MarketLiquidityProgressionDesign.md): 160 sale stock for each bulk commodity, 20 for each other ordinary commodity, replenishment over 1,200 simulation seconds, and unrestricted sale acceptance under existing access/hold rules. Stock capacity limits purchases, not the amount the station will buy from the player. Restricted goods retain their current access checks.

No new key/digit bindings, equipment, cargo capacity, prices, fees, demand model, stock tuning, saved fields, public bulk API command, bridge capability, or agent runner. Data lots, archive actions, living-seed papers and people remain outside the ordinary commodity composer. No work on unrelated backlog outcomes. The #55 document's older publication status is historical; current source is the integration baseline.

## Player interaction

Keep the existing table and its action order. Add one compact **Bulk trade** composer after the existing market/archive content, outside the six-column grid. It has a native, labelled commodity selector synchronized with `ui.marketSel`, a labelled whole-unit quantity field (initially 1), Buy Max and Sell All presets, and explicit Buy and Sell confirmation buttons. Existing arrow selection updates the composer when focus is outside native controls. Selecting another commodity clears its old confirmation and resets quantity to 1.

**Buy Max sets the quantity; it does not buy. Sell All sets the selected commodity's held quantity; it does not liquidate other goods.** Each preset focuses its corresponding confirmation button. Thus a normal full load takes Buy Max then Buy; unloading the same selected commodity takes Sell All then Sell. No per-chunk confirmation is required while the confirmed quote remains valid. A transaction is never triggered by merely typing, selecting, observing, or refreshing.

Show beside the field:

- Selected commodity and access status; available stock, held units and total used/capacity.
- Buy and sell executable unit quotes, their quantity totals and resulting cash/hold, with the unavailable side's precise reason.
- Confirm buttons including commodity, side, quantity and total, e.g. `Buy 160 Provisions · 16,000 UU`. At 100 UU/unit, enough cash, an empty 160-unit hold and 160 stock, the purchase preview is 160 units / 16,000 UU / hold 160 of 160.
- For quantities over the per-order limit: plain text `Processed in 2 orders. If the quote changes, the remainder stops for review.` The total is explicitly the total **at the displayed unit quote**; it is not a promise to silently accept a different later quote.

The field accepts decimal digits representing a positive safe integer. Empty, zero, negative, fraction, exponent, nonfinite or unsafe values disable confirmation with an inline message. Preserve the entered text while editing; never silently truncate, round or clamp an explicit request. A quantity above money, room, stock or held goods stays visible with the relevant limit and disabled side. Presets choose a valid quantity; a zero maximum produces a reason and no actionable zero-unit order. Disable the unavailable side, not the whole composer when the other side is usable.

At fixed state, the bulk buy quantity is:

`min(whole stock available, max(0, floor(cargoCapacity - cargoUsed)), max(0, floor(credits / buyUnit)))`

This is **not capped at 99**. Sell All is the whole held quantity of the selected allowed commodity, independent of retail stock/capacity. Access refusal sets both maxima to zero. Use the shared executable quote and `marketSupplyAt` projection. Invalid/nonfinite/negative units or totals make that side unavailable. For a valid zero buy quote, cash imposes no additional limit; stock and hold still do. Total and resulting cash arithmetic must remain safe/finite.

## Quote and execution contract

Use a read-only internal bulk preview, distinct from the public per-order availability. Proposed local interface:

`previewBulkTrade({ commodity, qty, side }) → { ok, reason, commodity, side, qty, unit, total, buyMaxTotal, sellMaxTotal, orderLimit, orderCount, creditsAfter, cargoUsedAfter }`

`qty` is always an explicit positive safe integer; presets compute it before calling this interface. `total = qty × unit` describes the current quote, not a simulation of future trust changes. `orderCount = ceil(qty / orderLimit)`. Failed previews contain a usable reason and maxima without presenting an executable total. The implementation may keep this helper nested beside `tradeFillUnit`; it must not become a second economic engine. Use ordinary legality, `cargoUsed`/`holdUnits` and supply helpers. Reads must not commit projected stock, grant cargo, bump trust, save, or arm confirmation state.

Retain the existing public trade arguments, version, tokens and 99-unit ceiling. Extract/reuse the current `tradeQty` limit rule if necessary, and enforce that same bound at the ordinary desk transaction boundary as well. This deliberately closes the current internal unlimited-quantity gap; it does not raise the public limit. Human small-lot calls, bulk chunks, `stationDesk.trade`, public `trade`, and any captured button path must validate the same individual order limit. Keep public `buyMax`/`sellMax` capped and preserve their existing field meanings; do not expose an uncapped 160 as a valid single agent order.

Implementation safety boundary: reject a bulk intent requiring more than 1,024 ordinary orders before any fill. Saved cargo counts can be much larger than an authored hold, so safe-integer arithmetic alone does not bound the synchronous work. This guard is far above ordinary freighter needs; it neither clips the requested quantity nor changes cargo/save data or individual-order limits.

When an edit or preset establishes a preview, retain an ephemeral immutable intent: commodity, side, requested quantity, displayed unit/total, station/service identity and the resource values underlying that preview. The rendered confirmation closure references that already displayed snapshot **before the click**. Clicking compares live state with the referenced snapshot; it must not capture a new live quote and thereby authorize an unseen price. A read-only capture must reuse the same intent, not refresh its authorization. No persistence or new event vocabulary is needed. The state machine is editing → preview ready → executing → complete or stopped. An intent can execute only once. Repeated keydown, double activation, an old captured closure and a second invocation during execution cannot replay it. A completed result requires a fresh edit/preset before another bulk confirmation; the small-lot controls remain independent.

Execution rules:

1. Revalidate that the player is still docked at this station/market and the selected commodity, access, unit quote and previewed resource values are still valid. If the preview is stale **before the first fill**, execute nothing, refresh the displayed preview with a changed-state notice and require another explicit confirmation. Never automatically grow Buy Max after stock replenishes or credits increase.
2. Submit sequential ordinary orders of `min(remaining, orderLimit)`, using `tryTrade` and its normal effects. A 160-unit freighter uses 99+61; never call the primitive with 160. Finish each order before considering the next. Recheck station/access, current quote and feasibility before each chunk. Reject invalid state rather than clipping a confirmed chunk.
3. Every next chunk must still use the confirmed unit quote. If a previous sale's fixer-trust effect changes that quote, stop before the next chunk, even when the new quote would pay more. Do not duplicate or predict fixer math in the preview. Preserve the completed order and its trust/stock/autosave effects exactly as sequential public-agent trades would.
4. Stop on the first refusal, state transition or quote change; never retry, skip, roll back a successful order, borrow cash, overfill, wait for replenishment, or resume automatically after reload. There is no cross-order atomic transaction. The existing ordinary order remains atomic.
5. Record each successful order's quantity, actual unit and total immediately. Aggregate completed quantity and actual UU; retain requested quantity, remaining quantity and stop reason. Do not infer full success from the final notice string or assume a successful first chunk means all requested units moved.

Receipt examples: `Bought 160 Provisions for 16,000 UU (2 orders).` Or `Sold 99 of 160 Restricted components for X UU. 61 remain: quote changed. Review the remainder.` An unchanged successful batch's receipt and cash/cargo deltas must exactly equal its preview. A stopped batch's receipt must exactly equal the sum of its completed previewed chunks and current cash/cargo delta; never label it as 160 completed or present the original full total as paid. Show completed UU separately from the refreshed remainder quote. A Review remainder action constructs a fresh preview; a second explicit Buy/Sell confirmation is required to continue.

Human and agent equality is defined for the **same initial state and ordered quantities**, including per-success trust changes and stock replenishment. A human 99+61 batch must have the same economics as an agent's 99 then 61 when quotes remain unchanged; where the quote changes, the human stops and an informed agent must inspect the new quote before its own next order. This issue does not remove the existing difference in trust earned by many small orders versus fewer large ones.

## Keyboard, rendering and access

Use native selector, input and button semantics with visible labels, focus indicators and DOM order matching the visual order. Tab/Shift+Tab reaches the selector, quantity, presets and confirmations; Enter/Space activates a focused button once. Enter in the quantity input does not submit a trade. Arrows in the selector/input retain their native behavior. Do not introduce a trading hotkey.

Before station shortcut routing, recognize native/editable focus and preserve its key ownership: entering `160`, selecting text with Ctrl+A, pasting, deleting, using arrows, or typing B/Q/W/A/S cannot launch, switch services, arm seed papers or trade. While a composer control owns focus, Escape cancels an uncommitted preview and returns focus to a labelled composer heading; a later Escape outside controls retains ordinary Back behavior. A focused native button's Space/Enter must survive `controls.js` default-prevention logic and must not trigger flight/fire input. Scope the controls guard to docked native UI ownership; preserve keyup cleanup, existing fire holds and the #11/#65 control boundaries. Verify actual focused elements in a browser.

Preserve commodity selection, quantity text, cursor/selection range and focused control across the one-second station refresh. Updating prices must not overwrite editing or implicitly confirm a new plan. Announce receipt/error changes through a polite live region once; avoid announcing every periodic quote refresh. Leaving Market, undocking, system load or recovery discards pending intents. Persist no composer state.

`captureView` must remain read-only and safe without DOM input APIs. Build text summaries and action labels through existing text-safe `h`/`btn` bindings; bind native listeners/focus only to real elements. Append composer buttons after existing market/archive actions so existing action indices do not shift. Preserve `VIEW_ROW_CAP=120` and `VIEW_TEXT_CAP=240`: confirmation labels must include the commodity/side/quantity/total within the existing text cap, and the separate captured action list must retain this information even when the rows truncate before the composer. Existing market fields supply unit and stock context; no new public fields or raised caps are needed. Do not assume only a human can call their closures: `stationAction` reaches the same validation, consumed-intent guard and chunk executor. Preserve expected-label stale checks. If an action stops without any fill, its result must be a refusal; a partial result must retain an unmistakable partial notice. Use a recognized refusal prefix/token or explicit internal outcome; do not silently report a refused bulk execution as success because a regex missed it. Public agents can continue using ordinary `trade` and need no input-field mutation capability.

Keep the six-column table and existing action wrapping. Composer controls wrap within the existing 560 px minimum panel without horizontal clipping; the panel can scroll vertically. Verify at 1280×720 and a viewport producing the 560 px panel, plus 200% browser zoom. Do not add content to the aim glass or move the agent badge. World/commodity/notice strings use `textContent` and safe attributes; no `innerHTML`.
