Wave 94 POWER ledger — verifier notes

Run:
  node out/w94/power/probe.mjs

Expect: WAVE94 POWER PASS

Live HUD / flight (manual):
1. Boot flight. Bottom-right side column shows a PWR bar (label PWR) next to Bio/POS.
2. Aim glass (reticle / lead) has no extra pip, ring, gauge, or PWR bar.
3. Tap Space with PWR below 15: afterburner must refuse (BURN stays READY / not BURNING).
4. Tap Space with PWR at 100: burn starts; PWR falls at 16/s while burning.
5. If PWR hits 0 during a burn, the burn cuts and cooldown starts.
6. Digit 5 psionic: each spawned bolt spends 10 PWR and 8 heat. Digit 1 cannon spends heat only.
7. Reduced-motion: PWR bar still fills; no extra pulse.

Binding:
  POWER = { max: 100, regenPerSec: 8, afterburnerPerSec: 16, afterburnerMin: 15 }
  WEAPONS.psionic.powerPerShot = 10
  Live pool only. Not a WORLD_FIELDS key. No class power field. No psi capacitor. No triad.
