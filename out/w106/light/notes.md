# Light — young reef shark

Compact fusiform body, one `grown_loft`. Blunt rounded snout toward -Z.
Thickest just aft of the gill basket. Short peduncle. Stern stops short of
the driver glow at z=+l*0.47 so the glow is a wake, not a nozzle.

Triangular first dorsal (`an.shark_dorsal`). Heterocercal caudal with a
longer upper lobe that is not a thresher scythe (`an.shark_caudal`). Paired
triangular pectorals (`an.shark_pectoral`) plus a tiny pelvic pair. Five
gill slits per side (`an.gill_slits`). Short brow crown, count 4, reduced
fan (`org.sensory_crown`). Pearl nacre on the back, indigo flanks, one
port scar, cyan vein fans in the gill/pectoral folds.

Stations are new `sf.fair` shark rings (round snout, mid-body peak). Seating
uses `surf_*` / `section` / `flank_x` / `top_y` / `span_ray` only.

Not copied from the old light.py body: manta wing pair, aft paddle pair,
tiny tail flukes, cephalic lobes, dolphin brow/shoulder/throat/crest lofts,
`fin_membrane`, paddle tips, or any squid/octopus/whale primitive.
